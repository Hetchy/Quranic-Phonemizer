# Imālah & Fath 

- Imālah is just in warsh.
- In Hafs, it only exists in one word, which is **مَجْرَاهَا**, in **Surat al-Hud**, verse **42**.
- Imālah is the inclination of the pronunciation of a final **alif** toward the sound of **yāʾ** and of a final **fatḥah** toward the sound of **kasrah**.
- Its mark is a large black dot beneath the letter.
- Fath is the plain, fully open pronunciation of a fat7a.
- Fath is the original state, whereas imālah cannot occur without one of the recognized causes. If that cause is absent, its effect -namely, the imālah- is absent as well.

- Next is an analysis of imālah and fatḥ in the Uthmanic King Fahd Qur'an dataset.

## Imālah

- Imālah has 2 types:

## **1. Imālah Kubrā ( الامالة الكبرى )**

- **Pronunciation:** The alif is read as 50% yā’ and 50% alif.
- **Notes :**
    - The pronunciation of imālah kubrā is similar to the accent grave è in French—for example: grève.
    - Note that in phonetics, the è symbol is represented as /ɛ/.
- **Occurrences: where imālah kubrā only is allowed :**

    | Word | Ref |
    | --- | --- |
    | **طَه۪ۖ** | 20:1 |

### **2. Imālah Soughra - Taklil (الامالة الصغرى -  تقليل )** 

- **Pronunciation:** The alif is read as 70% alif and 30% yā’.
- It's between al-fatḥ and imālah kubra.
- **Notes :**
