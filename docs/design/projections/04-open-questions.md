# 04 - Open questions

Status: **open**. Each needs an owner decision before the part of the contract
it touches can be built. None blocks the rest.

## 1. What decides the colour of a ghunnah?

An ikhfaa before an istilaa letter is taught heavy. The sound is there, hosted
by the ikhfaa itself, and `emphatic` on it is the field the colour would go in.
What is missing is the rule: weight is decided by the letter of the unit it
fires on, and the noon whose hum this is has no weight of its own. Colouring it
needs a predicate over the *following* letter, and no rule takes one.

Either a rule learns to read the next letter, which is a new shape for weight;
or the heavy ghunnah is a fact the model does not carry and
[02-gate](02-gate.md)'s converse row for it is withdrawn.
