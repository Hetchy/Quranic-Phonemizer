# 06 - The seen/sad khilaf

Status: resolved by the granular Hafs khilaf implementation.

The four selectable word forms have separate scalar variant IDs because their
defaults are not uniform:

- `seen_sad_yabsut`
- `seen_sad_bastah`
- `seen_sad_al_musaytirun`
- `seen_sad_bimusaytir`

Each ID selects `seen` or `saad` for its complete covered word form. A seen
selection produces plain s and no tafkheem occurrence. A saad selection
produces emphatic s, adds tafkheem, and makes an adjacent fatha emphatic where
applicable.

The small-seen script mark still has an independent sakt meaning. The four
sakt locations are not selectable variants and remain governed by their sakt
evidence.

The public options, defaults, references, Score effects, and research sources
are maintained in [the Hafs variants catalogue](../hafs/variants.md).
