# Exceptional Tajweed Rules

(Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim – Shāṭibiyyah pathway)

These rules are not generalizable. They occur only in specific words or positions and are transmitted through riwāyah.

# Ishmām (Lip Rounding Without Sound)

## Definition

الإشارة إلى الضم بضم الشفتين بعد النطق بالحرف ساكنًا دون صوت

A silent lip-rounding gesture after pronouncing a consonant with sukoon, indicating that the original vowel was ḍammah.

## Condition

يكون بعد حرف ساكن أُدغم، للدلالة على أن أصله مضموم.
Occurs after complete idghām where the original underlying vowel was ḍammah.

## Duration

No sound. It is a visual gesture only.

Occurrence in Ḥafṣ

Surah Yusuf (12:11):

مَا لَكَ لَا تَأْمَنَّا عَلَىٰ يُوسُفَ

## Notes

The two nūns are fully merged (idghām with ghunnah).

After pronouncing the merged consonant, the lips are rounded briefly.

It is seen but not heard.

This is the only occurrence in Ḥafṣ (Shāṭibiyyah).

# Rawm (Partial Vowel at Pause)
## Definition

الإتيان ببعض الحركة (حوالي ثلثها) عند الوقف بصوت خفي

Pronouncing approximately one-third of the vowel sound when stopping.

## Condition

Occurs when stopping on:

يكون عند الوقف على المرفوع أو المجرور

A word ending with ḍammah

A word ending with kasrah

Not applied to fatḥah.

## Duration

Approximately 1/3 of the vowel.

## Example

نَسْتَعِينُ
When stopping, a slight audible ḍammah may remain.

## Notes

It is heard but not seen.

It is a stopping variation, not a separate phoneme.

Not applied to open fatḥah endings.

# Sakt (Brief Pause Without Breath)

## Definition

قطع الصوت زمنًا يسيرًا بدون تنفس مع نية الوصل

A brief pause without breathing while intending continuation.

## Condition

يكون في مواضع محددة فقط في رواية حفص

Occurs only in transmitted positions in Ḥafṣ.

## Duration

Very short pause (shorter than waqf). No breath is taken.

## The Four Well-Known Positions

Al-Kahf 18:1 → عِوَجَا ۜ قَيِّمًا

Yā-Sīn 36:52 → مَنۢ بَعَثَنَا مِن مَّرْقَدِنَا

Al-Qiyāmah 75:27 → وَقِيلَ مَنْ ۜ رَاقٍ

Al-Muṭaffifīn 83:14 → كَلَّا بَلْ ۜ رَانَ

## Notes

No breathing during sakt.

It is shorter than a full stop (waqf).

Not extendable beyond these positions.

# Tashīl al-Hamzah (Softening of the Second Hamzah)
## Definition

النطق بالهمزة الثانية بين الهمزة المحققة وحرف المد

Pronouncing the second hamzah in a softened manner, between a full hamzah and a vowel sound.

## Condition

يكون عند التقاء همزتين في كلمة واحدة

Occurs when two hamzahs meet in one word.

## Occurrence

ءَأَعْجَمِيٌّ (Fussilat 41:44)

## Notes

The second hamzah is neither fully articulated nor completely dropped.

Limited to specific transmitted cases.

| Sakt   | 4 positions        | Yes   | No   |
| Tashīl | Specific words     | Yes   | No   |
